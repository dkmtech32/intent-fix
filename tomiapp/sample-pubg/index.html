Quick answers

Is PUBG MOBILE free to download?

Yes — the base game downloads free. Optional in-game purchases exist for cosmetics and passes; they are not required to play Classic matches.

Android and iOS?

Both platforms are supported. Use the official store listing for your device after you tap Download.

Do I need a controller?

No. Touch + optional gyro covers most players. Controllers are supported on some devices if you prefer sticks.

What should I play first?

TDM or a casual Classic Duo — learn loot paths and zone timing before grinding Ranked.
